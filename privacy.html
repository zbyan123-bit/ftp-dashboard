Privacy policy: FTP dashboard

FTP dashboard is a personal training dashboard used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own WHOOP recovery, sleep and cycle data and the owner's own Strava rides (time, distance, power and heart rate).

What it stores

It stores a summary of that data (for example daily recovery score, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep time, and ride duration and power) in this dashboard so the owner can see training trends. It does not store GPS or location data. Login tokens are stored encrypted.

Sharing

The data is not sold or shared with anyone. The dashboard page is published by its owner.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in WHOOP or Strava settings, which stops all further access.
